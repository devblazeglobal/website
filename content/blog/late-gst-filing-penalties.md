A GST-registered business must file a GST return (Form F5) and pay any GST due within 1 month after the end of each accounting period. Most businesses file quarterly.

## What is the penalty for filing late?

- **S$200** as soon as the due date passes.
- A further **S$200** for each completed month the return remains outstanding.
- Up to **S$10,000** for each outstanding return.

IRAS can also issue an estimated assessment for the period, and the GST on that estimate must be paid.

## What is the penalty for paying late?

- A **5%** penalty applies to GST not paid by the due date.
- If the GST is still unpaid 60 days later, a further **2%** applies for each completed month, up to **50%** of the unpaid GST.

The two penalties are separate. A return that is filed late and paid late attracts both.

## Can you ask for more time?

Yes. Requests to extend the filing date must reach IRAS at least 5 working days before the due date. A late request does not stop the late filing penalty.

## How to stay on time

- Keep sales and purchase records up to date every month, not only at quarter end.
- Reconcile the GST accounts before filing, so the F5 matches your books.
- Set the filing and payment dates for the year as soon as you register.
