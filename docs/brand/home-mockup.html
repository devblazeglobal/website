What a new company needs

  1. 1

    Choose a business structure

    Sole proprietorship, partnership, LLP or private limited. We explain each and recommend one.

  2. 2

    Incorporate the company

    Registration with ACRA, company constitution and the first directors' resolutions.

  3. 3

    Appoint a company secretary

    Required within 6 months of incorporation. We provide a qualified person.

  4. 4

    Set up the books

    Bank, card and cash records on Xero, matched as they come in.

  5. 5

    Plan the first financial year

    Tax treatment for newly incorporated companies, GST registration if required, and the first annual return.
