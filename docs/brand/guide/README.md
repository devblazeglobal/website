Blaze Global is a Singapore accounting, business advisory and corporate secretarial firm (UEN 202011886G, ACRA Registered Filing Agent FA20210247, 14 Robinson Road). Clients are founders and directors of Singapore start-ups and established SMEs. The brand reads as a CBD professional firm: navy and gold, a classical serif, a lot of room.

Design reference: harvey.ai for type, spacing and restraint. Flow reference: lawhive.co.uk for the order a visitor moves through a page.

- Use `Logos/blaze-global-wordmark-navy-1200.png` on `paper`, `surface` and white documents (invoices, letters, PDFs).

## Colour

- `navy` and `gold` are the brand. Everything else supports them.
- Page ground is `paper`. Cards and fields are `surface`. Alternate long pages with `surface-alt` bands.
- Full-bleed `navy` sections: the header, the hero, the call-to-action band, the footer. Text on them is `on-navy` and `on-navy-muted`; accents are `gold`.
- Backgrounds are navy, white and grey only: `navy`, `surface` (white), `paper` and `surface-alt` (greys).
- Gold is a minor accent: the logo, WhatsApp buttons (`gold` fill, `on-gold` text, WhatsApp glyph), ticks on navy, eyebrow labels (`gold-text` on light), small tags. Review stars follow Google's own yellow (#FBBC04). Never a background band and never text on light grounds (1.4:1).
- The primary button is `navy` with `on-navy` text; on navy grounds it is white with `ink` text. One primary button per screen area.
- Hairlines (`line`) separate; shadows are not used.

## Typography

- Headings: `serif` (Newsreader), weight 400, title case for H1 and H2. Display styles: `display-xl` (home H1 only), `display-lg` (page H1), `display-md` (H2).
- Everything else: `sans` (Helvetica). `title` for card titles, `lead` under an H1, `body` for copy, `small` for captions and the footer, `label` for uppercase eyebrows.
- One H1 per page. The H1 names the service and the place: "Corporate secretarial services in Singapore".
- Keep running text to 68 characters a line (`post-max` for articles).

## Spacing and layout

- Spacing doubles: `space-xs` 8, `space-sm` 16, `space-md` 32, `space-lg` 64, `space-xl` 128, `space-2xl` 160 at desktop. Scale by 0.875 under 1025px, 1.125 at 1445px, 1.25 at 1730px.
- Every section gets `space-xl` top and bottom. Blocks inside a section are `space-lg` apart. A heading sits `space-md` above its content.
- Header is `header-height` (72px). Content sits in `content-max` (1092px); bands run to `page-max`.
- Hero layout (from harvey.ai): H1 left, lead and button right, on one row from 1025px; stacked below.
- Radii are small: `radius-md` buttons, `radius-lg` cards, `radius-xl` large media. No pills except tags.
- Build for phone, tablet and desktop at the same time. On phones a fixed bar carries Call and WhatsApp.

## Page flow

Every page follows this order (from lawhive.co.uk):

1. Hero: H1, one-line lead, WhatsApp button, call button.
2. Proof strip: ISCA, ACRA filing agent, Chartered Accountants Worldwide, Xero partner.
3. Services: one card per service, each linking to its page.
4. How it works: three numbered steps (message, scoping call, onboarding).
5. People: the accountants, with name, qualification and photo (photos to be supplied).
6. Testimonial: one quote in `quote`, name and company.
7. Call-to-action band: `navy`, one line and one button.
8. Footer.

## Voice

- Plain, factual, courteous. State the fact, then stop.
- Headings name the thing: "Our services", "Fees", "Contact". No questions as headings, no slogans.
- Numbers over adjectives: "within 14 days", not "fast".
- No exclamation marks. No "unlock", "seamless", "best-in-class".
- Write to the client as "you", the firm as "we".
